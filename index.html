How To Use

Quick Start

  1. Login: Click "Login with Discord" to connect your account
  2. Link Profile: Select your team and player name when prompted
  3. View Schedule: See your upcoming weeks and which teams you're playing
  4. Confirm Availability: Click "I Can Play" or "Can't Make It" for each week
  5. Check Team Status: See who else on your team has confirmed

Team Announcements

Any team member can send an announcement to your team's Discord:

  1. Set up a webhook in Team Settings (one-time setup)
  2. Click "Announce to Team" on any week card
  3. Your team's Discord channel will get a message asking everyone to confirm

Need a Sub?

If you can't make it, mark yourself as unavailable. The team will see how many subs are needed.
